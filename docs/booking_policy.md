# Booking Policy

## Cancellation Rules
- Reservations can be cancelled **free of charge** if the cancellation is made **at least 24 hours before the scheduled check‑in time**.
- Cancellations made **less than 24 hours** prior to check‑in incur a **50 % fee** (only half of the reservation amount is refunded).

## Late Checkout Policy
- Guests may request a late checkout.
- A flat fee of **$30 per extra hour** is applied for any checkout time after **12 PM**.
- The fee is calculated as `hours × $30` where *hours* is the number of additional hours requested.

## Room Number Constraints
- When making a reservation, the system validates the room number.
- Only room numbers **between 1 and 10 inclusive** are accepted.
- If a number outside this range is entered, the system prints **"Invalid Room Number! Room number must be between 1 and 10 strictly."** and does not proceed with the reservation.
